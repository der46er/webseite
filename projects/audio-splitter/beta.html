How to use

  • 1. Select an audio file from your device.
  • 2. Zoom: Use your mouse wheel over the track, or pinch on mobile to zoom in/out.
  • 3. Trim: Drag the yellow box edges to select the part you want to keep.
  • 4. Split: Enter a target MB size and the tool will slice the audio into pieces.
  • 5. Hit Export. Audio processes locally on your device!
