How to use

  • 1. Choose your background flag or upload a custom one.
  • 2. Upload a photo.
  • 3. Double-click the image preview to enter edit mode.
  • 4. Click and drag to move your photo. It locks to the edges.
  • 5. Use the +/- buttons, mouse wheel, or pinch on mobile to zoom.
  • 6. Double-click again to lock it, then download!
