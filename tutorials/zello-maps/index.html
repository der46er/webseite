Zello & Google Maps: Group Guide

Step-by-step guide for group radio and real-time location sharing

1. Zello (Group Walkie-Talkie)

This is what the Zello app icon looks like:

Look for this orange icon with the circle in the middle on your phone screen.

Step 1: Download Zello to your Smartphone

For Apple iPhone

Tap the image above or search for 'Zello' in the blue App Store. Tap Get.

For Android (Samsung, Xiaomi, etc.)

Tap the image above or search for 'Zello' in the colorful Play Store. Tap Install.

Step 2: Initial Setup & Permissions (Zello Version 7.15)

  • 1
    Create an account (first time only)

    Open Zello. On the welcome screen, tap Create account (or Create Zello account).

    • Username: Zello requires a username without spaces (e.g. User1234). Choose something recognizable like your first name and city (e.g. MariaLinz or Peter4020). If already taken, just add numbers at the end.
    • Password: Enter a password (at least 6 characters) that is easy for you to remember.
    • Email: Enter your standard email address (in case you need to reset your password).
  • 2
    Allow Microphone (Important!)

    A prompt appears: 'Allow Zello to access microphone?' Tap 'Allow' or 'While using the app'.

Setting up the shared radio group

  • A
    One person creates the channel once:

    In Zello, tap Channels → + → 'Create new channel'. Choose a name (e.g. TravelGroup1234), set to 'Password protected', pick a password (e.g. sun123) and tap Create.

  • B
    Everyone else joins the channel:

    Open Zello → Channels → + → 'Find channel' → type name, enter password, and tap Join.

  • C
    Talking in the group (Walkie-Talkie style):
    Hold &
    Speak

    1. Daumen auf den Kreis legen & fest gedrückt halten. 2. Satz sprechen. 3. Danach Daumen sofort loslassen!

Test Tip: Call the test contact echo in your contacts: hold the button and speak – echo plays back your voice to verify.

2. View Group Locations on Map (Google Maps)

This is what the Google Maps app icon looks like:

Look for this colorful map pin icon labeled Maps on your phone screen.

  • 1
    Open Google Maps

    Tap the colorful map pin icon Maps on your phone.

  • 2
    Open the profile menu

    At the top right of the search bar, tap the circle with your initial (or profile picture).

  • 3
    Select Location sharing

    In the menu, tap Location sharing (next to the small pin icon).

  • 4
    Start sharing your location

    Tap the blue button Share location (or tap + New share at top left).

  • 5
    Set the duration

    Choose how long others can see your real-time location:

    • Tap (+) to set the duration, e.g. 'For 4 hours' or 'For 8 hours' (ideal for day trips).
    • Or choose 'Until you turn this off' for continuous sharing.
  • 6
    Send link to WhatsApp group chat

    In the bottom tray, tap the green WhatsApp icon (or Copy link). Select your group chat and send.

  • 7
    Make everyone visible: 'Share back'

    To ensure every participant sees each other:

    • Everyone taps the shared link in WhatsApp.
    • Google Maps opens. A blue bar appears: 'Share location back with...'.
    • Everyone taps this bar. Now all avatars are simultaneously visible on the map.
  • 8
    Stop sharing

    After the trip: Google Maps → top-right profile icon → Location sharing → tap share → Stop.
